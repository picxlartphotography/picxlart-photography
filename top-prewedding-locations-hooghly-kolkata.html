Top Pre-Wedding Shoot Locations in Hooghly & Kolkata: Heritage, Nature & Waterfronts

By Picxlart Team July 2026 5 Min Read

A pre-wedding shoot is more than just taking pretty pictures—it is about capturing your real chemistry in a backdrop that matches your personality. Whether you love the regal vibe of colonial architecture, peaceful riverbanks, or vibrant urban landscapes, West Bengal offers some of the most romantic locations for a pre-wedding shoot.

Based in Serampore, Hooghly, our team at Picxlart Photography has traveled across Kolkata and Hooghly district to curate this comprehensive guide. Here are the top pre-wedding shoot locations across Hooghly and Kolkata, categorized into paid, free, natural, and heritage spots.

Part 1: Scenic Pre-Wedding Locations in Hooghly

Hooghly is a hidden gem for couples who want authentic European colonial heritage and serene natural riverfronts away from city noise.

1. Serampore Rajbari Paid Spot

If you dream of a royal traditional Bengali look, Serampore Rajbari is a fantastic choice. It blends classic aristocratic architecture with privacy and convenience.

  • Key Feature: Dedicated makeup rooms provided for outfit changes.
  • Costing: Charged on an hourly basis (every 2 hours slot).
  • Best For: Saree/Kurta traditionals, Royal bridal portraits.

2. Serampore Jolkol & Serampore Ghat Free / Public

Located right next to the historic Serampore College, the Jolkol area and Serampore Ghat offer a rustic, old-world riverside charm. Nearby restored colonial architecture (like Denmark Tavern) adds a touch of Danish legacy to your frames.

3. Hooghly Imambara (Chinsurah) Public Entry Fee

Famous for its grand Victorian clock towers, endless arched corridors, and high balconies overlooking the Hooghly River. It gives a royal, timeless aesthetic.

  • Timings: 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM.

4. Hangseshwari Temple (Bansberia) Public

Known for its striking 13-towered lotus-inspired architecture and the adjacent stone Anant Vasudeva temple. Perfect for traditional artistic shots.

5. Bandel Church / Basilica of the Holy Rosary Public

Established in 1599, offering European stone facades, quiet manicured lawns, and a peaceful ambiance.

  • Timings: 8:00 AM to 4:50 PM.

6. Chandannagar Strand & Sabujdeep Scenic / Nature

Chandannagar Strand: A long paved promenade along the river with vintage French-colonial lamps—unbeatable for sunset romantic silhouettes.

Sabujdeep: An island surrounded by water with lush green trees, accessible by a short boat ride—ideal for nature lovers.

Hooghly Rural Villages: Moving slightly into rural Hooghly opens up lush green paddy fields, mud houses, and natural ponds for authentic Bengali village romance.

Part 2: Iconic Pre-Wedding Locations in Kolkata

Kolkata brings a perfect blend of high-energy urban vibes, historic monuments, and luxury resorts.

7. Princep Ghat & Hooghly Riverfront Free / Public

The iconic Greek-style white pillars of Princep Ghat with the Vidyasagar Setu in the background make this Kolkata's #1 shoot spot. You can also rent a traditional wooden boat for romantic river shots at golden hour.

8. Eco Park & Eco Island (New Town) Paid Entry

Offering huge variety in a single place—from modern glass bridges and green tea gardens to replicas of the 7 Wonders of the World.

9. Sovabazar Rajbari & Bow Barracks Heritage Vibe

For vintage yellow taxis, narrow alleys, red-brick European houses (Bow Barracks), or grand traditional Thakur Dalan (Sovabazar Rajbari), Kolkata's North district is unmatched.

10. Luxury Resorts (Bawali Rajbari, Ibiza Resort, Vedic Village) Paid Booking

If you prefer complete privacy, changing room luxury, and royal aesthetics, private resort properties around Kolkata provide breathtaking photo opportunities.

Tips for Planning Your Pre-Wedding Shoot

To get the best out of your session:

  • Choose Outfits Wisely: Match light pastel colors for greenery/nature spots and bright silk sarees/gowns for heritage Rajbaris.
  • Timing is Key: Early morning (6:30 AM to 9:00 AM) or late afternoon (4:00 PM to 6:00 PM) offers soft, glowing lighting.
  • Plan Travel Routes: If you reside around Serampore/Hooghly, combining Serampore Rajbari with Chandannagar Strand saves time and travel fatigue.
