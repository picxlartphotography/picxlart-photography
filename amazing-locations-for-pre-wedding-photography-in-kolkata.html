Kolkata Location Guide

25 Amazing Locations For Pre Wedding Photography In Kolkata

By Picxlart Team July 2026 10 Min Read

Kolkata, known as the City of Joy, seamlessly weaves timeless heritage with modern elegance. From grand colonial monuments and narrow vintage streets to tranquil lakes, art hubs, and luxury resorts, the city offers an endless palette for couples planning their pre-wedding photoshoot.

Whether you dream of a romantic sunset ride on a traditional wooden boat or a vintage retro tram ride, our team at Picxlart Photography has put together this ultimate guide featuring all 25 top pre-wedding photoshoot locations in Kolkata.

Part 1: Iconic Heritage, Riverfronts & Public Landmarks

1. Princep Ghat & River Ganges Free Outdoor Spot

The Corinthian white pillars of Princep Ghat set against the iconic Vidyasagar Setu suspension bridge offer Kolkata's most romantic backdrop. Hiring a traditional wooden boat on the Ganges at sunset unlocks breathtaking golden-hour couple silhouettes.

  • Best Timing: Early morning (6:00 AM – 8:00 AM) or Golden Hour Sunset (4:30 PM – 6:00 PM).
  • Ideal Vibe: Romantic, traditional saree & dhoti, flowing western gowns.

2. Smaranika Tram Museum Heritage Vibe / Entry Fee

Located near CTC Maidan, this air-conditioned vintage tram car and museum allows couples to capture classic 1970s Calcuttan romance among wooden seats, vintage tickets, and nostalgic interiors.

3. Acharya Jagadish Chandra Bose Botanical Garden Lush Greenery

Spanning over 270 acres in Shibpur (Howrah), this botanical paradise features giant Amazonian lily pads, arched mahogany avenues, and the world-famous Great Banyan Tree for peaceful, nature-centric photography.

4. Rabindra Sarobar Lake Nature / Serene

A tranquil artificial lake in South Kolkata surrounded by thick green canopies, stone-paved walkways, iron bridges, and vintage lamp posts. Perfect for casual morning couple portraits.

5. Eco Park & Eco Island (New Town) Paid Entry

Spanning 480 acres, Eco Park is a modern outdoor studio featuring replicas of the Seven Wonders of the World, tea garden landscapes, glass bridges, and lakeside walkways—allowing multiple themes in a single trip.

6. Mallick Ghat Flower Market Vibrant Street Vibe

Nestled beneath Howrah Bridge, Asia's largest wholesale flower market offers thousands of bright yellow marigold garlands and intense street culture for vibrant, artistic documentary-style pre-wedding frames.

7. Bow Barracks Vintage European Vibe

Tucked behind Bowbazar, these red-brick Anglo-Indian colonial apartments, green window shutters, and narrow brick alleys exude old-world European charm and retro aesthetic.

8. Victoria Memorial Grounds Ticketed Entry

The imposing white Makrana marble palace, sprawling green lawns, reflection pools, and manicured flowerbeds deliver British-era architectural grandeur and royal elegance.

9. Kolkata Maidan Open Grasslands

Vast green meadows with grazing horses, vintage tram lines cutting through, and soft misty morning light—unbeatable for natural, airy, and romantic candid portraits.

10. South Park Street Cemetery Gothic / Heritage

For couples who appreciate artistic Gothic architecture, moss-covered stone obelisks, towering ancient trees, and dramatic shadowy light play right in Park Street.

Part 2: Cultural Royal Palaces & Literary Nostalgia

11. Jorasanko Thakur Bari Cultural Heritage

The ancestral mansion of Nobel Laureate Rabindranath Tagore. Featuring classic red-brick verandahs, grand courtyards, and traditional aristocratic Bengali heritage.

12. The Rajbari Bawali Luxury Heritage Resort

A beautifully restored 300-year-old zamindari palace located near Budge Budge. Grand brick arches, vintage courtyards, antique furniture, and royal pond-side setups make it Kolkata's top luxury pre-wedding resort.

13. Howrah Bridge (Rabindra Setu) Iconic Landmark

The iconic steel cantilever bridge spanning the Hooghly River. Shots taken from nearby ghats or ferry decks capture the quintessential soul of Kolkata.

14. Indian Museum Grounds Historic Corridor

The oldest museum in India features grand white Ionic columns, high arched corridors, and quiet inner quadrangles ideal for architectural fashion portraiture.

15. Marble Palace (North Kolkata) Special Permission Required

A 19th-century palatial mansion in Chorbagan adorned with Italian marble walls, antique sculptures, Victorian chandeliers, and private manicured gardens.

Part 3: Artistic Streets, Sacred Spaces & Modern Horizons

16. St. Paul’s Cathedral Indo-Gothic Heritage

An architectural marvel featuring Indo-Gothic spires, stained glass windows, and quiet green surroundings right adjacent to Victoria Memorial.

17. Kumartuli Artistic Street

The historic potters' neighborhood along the riverbank where artisan sculptors craft clay idols of gods and goddesses. Offers raw, artistic, and culturally rich storytelling frames.

18. College Street (Boi Para) Literary Nostalgia

The world's largest second-hand book market. Stacks of old books, vintage coffee houses, yellow taxis, and historic university buildings create a nostalgic setting for intellectual, book-loving couples.

19. Sovabazar Rajbari Traditional Thakur Dalan

Famous for its magnificent traditional open courtyard (Thakur Dalan), classic colonnades, and royal heritage—perfect for traditional silk saree and dhoti-panjabi portraits.

20. Nicco Park Fun & Playful Vibe

Ideal for energetic, fun-loving couples wanting joyful candid photos on amusement rides, merry-go-rounds, and vibrant lakeside backgrounds.

21. Vidyasagar Setu Lookout (Second Hooghly Bridge) Modern Skyline

Offers dramatic modern cable-stayed bridge architecture and panoramic sunset river views across the Kolkata-Howrah skyline.

22. Birla Mandir Marble Architecture

An intricately carved white Rajasthani-style marble temple delivering divine elegance and pure traditional aesthetic.

23. Belur Math Peaceful Riverside

Situated on the west bank of Hooghly river, featuring peaceful green lawns, spiritual architecture, and calm riverbank seating.

24. Chintamoni Kar Bird Sanctuary (Narendrapur) Dense Forest Trail

A lush green bird sanctuary in South Kolkata filled with tall bamboo groves, orchard trails, and quiet forest paths for nature lovers.

25. Calcutta Bungalow (Shyambazar) Restored 1920s Heritage Townhouse

A restored 1920s vintage townhouse with retro Bengali decor, courtyard verandas, antique furniture, and classic 20th-century Calcuttan nostalgia.
