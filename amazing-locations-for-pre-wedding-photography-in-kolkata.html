Kolkata Location Guide

Amazing Locations For Pre Wedding Photography In Kolkata

By Picxlart Team June 2026 6 Min Read

Kolkata, known as the City of Joy, seamlessly weaves timeless heritage with modern elegance. From grand colonial monuments and narrow vintage streets to tranquil lakes and luxury resorts, the city offers an endless palette for couples planning their pre-wedding photoshoot.

Whether you dream of a romantic sunset ride on a traditional wooden boat or a majestic royal bridal portrait, our team at Picxlart Photography has put together this definitive guide to help you select the ultimate pre-wedding photoshoot spots in Kolkata.

Part 1: Iconic Outdoor & Heritage Public Spots

These locations highlight Kolkata's original cultural essence, architecture, and romantic riverfront views.

1. Princep Ghat & Hooghly Riverfront Free Outdoor Spot

The Greek-restoration pillars of Princep Ghat set against the majestic Vidyasagar Setu suspension bridge offer Kolkata’s most iconic backdrop. Golden hour here is pure magic for romantic silhouettes.

  • Special Highlight: Rent a traditional wooden boat on the river Ganges for cinematic couple portraits.
  • Best Time: Early morning (6:00 AM – 8:00 AM) to avoid crowds or late afternoon sunset.

2. Victoria Memorial & Maidan Ticketed Entry

Featuring sprawling green gardens, calm water bodies, and the imposing white marble monument, Victoria Memorial delivers timeless royal grandeur. Nearby Maidan offers vast grassy fields and tram tracks.

  • Vibe: Classic, aristocratic, and grand romantic frames.

3. North Kolkata Heritage Streets (Bow Barracks & Sovabazar) Free Public Access

For couples who love vintage nostalgia, North Kolkata is unbeatable. Red-brick colonial mansions in Bow Barracks, iconic yellow taxis, narrow alleys, and ancient thakur dalans provide rich cultural storytelling.

4. Eco Park & Eco Island (New Town) Paid Entry

Spanning over 480 acres, Eco Park is a modern paradise offering incredible diversity in a single trip—from tea gardens and lakeside walkways to replicas of the Seven Wonders of the World.

5. Rabindra Sarobar Lake & Botanical Garden Nature / Serene

If you prefer lush greenery, calm lakes, giant hanging tree roots, and peaceful walking tracks away from city noise, Rabindra Sarobar and Acharya Jagadish Chandra Bose Indian Botanic Garden (Shibpur) offer dreamlike natural frames.

Part 2: Royal Rajbaris & Luxury Private Resorts

If you prefer complete privacy, dedicated changing/makeup rooms, and curated regal sets, Kolkata's heritage resorts are second to none.

6. The Rajbari Bawali Paid Heritage Resort

A beautifully restored 300-year-old architectural masterpiece. Featuring grand brick courtyards, vintage columns, and antique décor, it is the ultimate destination for luxury royal pre-wedding shoots.

7. Ibiza The Fern Resort & Spa Paid Resort

Spread across lush green acres with private pools, serene lakes, and modern luxury architecture—ideal for elegant gown/tuxedo concepts and romantic poolside sessions.

8. Vedic Village Spa Resort Paid Resort

Combines rustic Bengali mud-house aesthetics with modern resort luxury. Perfect for couples seeking a peaceful, green countryside feel right on the edge of Kolkata.

Pro Tips for Your Pre-Wedding Shoot in Kolkata

  • Shoot Permission: Public heritage places like Princep Ghat or Victoria Memorial grounds require early morning timing to avoid heavy public crowds. Private resorts require advance booking.
  • Outfit Strategy: Wear vibrant traditional wear (Silk Saree / Sherwani) for Rajbari and heritage street shoots, and flowy western gowns/suits for lakeside or modern resort locations.
  • Best Season: October to March offers pleasant weather, clear skies, and comfortable outdoor conditions.
