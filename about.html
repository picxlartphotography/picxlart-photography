Timeless Moments, Captured for Eternity with Passion & Artistic Excellence

Our Brand Story

At Picxlart, we believe every special moment deserves to be preserved with creativity, passion, and perfection. Since 2016, we have been capturing timeless memories through artistic storytelling, cinematic visuals, and premium-quality photography services.

We specialize in Wedding Photography, Cinematic Wedding Films, Candid Photography, Pre-Wedding Shoots, Bridal Portraits, and Destination Weddings across West Bengal and India.

From grand weddings and cinematic wedding films to candid photography, pre-wedding shoots, bridal portraits, destination weddings, corporate events, birthdays, rice ceremonies (Annaprashan), baby showers, anniversaries, housewarming celebrations, family functions, school and college events, our experienced team is committed to delivering premium photography and cinematic storytelling tailored to every occasion.

Based in West Bengal, Picxlart proudly serves clients across Kolkata, Serampore, Hooghly, Durgapur, Asansol, Siliguri, Kharagpur, and destinations throughout India. Whether your event is close to home or in another state, our team is always ready to travel to capture your story with the same passion and professionalism.
