Top 8 Wedding Sherwani Showrooms in Kolkata: A Guide for the Dapper Groom

By Picxlart Team August 2026 7 Min Read

Your wedding day is a once-in-a-lifetime milestone, and your attire should reflect your personality, class, and elegance. Finding the ideal Sherwani in Kolkata can sometimes feel overwhelming given the countless choices available. To simplify your journey, we have curated a list of the top 8 wedding sherwani showrooms in Kolkata that promise a royal groom look.

1. Manyavar

A trusted household name for ethnic menswear, Manyavar bridges contemporary trends with traditional silhouettes. Offering a vast range of traditional Indian, Indo-western, and formal options, they ensure every groom looks regal. With an extensive retail network and robust in-house manufacturing, they remain a top destination for wedding shopping.

Picxlart Photography Note: The royal embroidery and regal color tones of a Manyavar Sherwani stand out brilliantly in close-up portrait shots and cinematic wedding films.

2. The Raymond Shop

A legendary name in Indian fashion for over nine decades, The Raymond Shop offers complete wardrobe solutions ranging from fine suiting fabrics to bespoke custom tailoring. Whether you want a timeless traditional look or a sharp western ceremonial suit, their unmatched quality makes them an evergreen choice.

Picxlart Photography Note: A sharp, perfectly tailored outfit from Raymond provides clean lines and sophisticated styling that look phenomenal in candid wedding moments.

3. The Arvind Store

Situated in Camac Street, The Arvind Store brings together premium shirting, suiting, and custom-made apparel under one roof. Known for new-age fabrics and modern cuts, they cater to discerning grooms looking for a blend of comfort and international flair.

Picxlart Photography Note: The rich fabric textures from Arvind catch ambient wedding lighting exquisitely, enhancing the depth of your event photographs.

4. Abhishek Ray

Based in Kolkata, Abhishek Ray is a premier designer brand specializing in exclusive groomswear. Offering an extensive blend of ethnic and western patterns crafted from the finest materials, they also provide quick custom-made outfits and tailoring modifications to ensure a flawless fit.

Picxlart Photography Note: Abhishek Ray's creative cuts and artistic flair create striking visual compositions during pre-wedding and reception shoots.

5. Tasva

Co-created with Tarun Tahiliani, Tasva redefines men's ethnic fashion by seamlessly blending rich Indian heritage with modern aesthetics. Their exquisite range of sherwanis, bandis, and Indo-western wear is crafted for the sophisticated groom who values both style and absolute ease.

Picxlart Photography Note: Tasva’s modern silhouettes and detailed craftsmanship complement our cinematic style of videography and storytelling seamlessly.

6. Anil Kumar Ajit Kumar Designer Wear

Boasting a dedicated team of over 200 skilled artisans, this renowned Kolkata store specializes in handcrafted, high-precision sherwanis and ethnic wear. If you have a custom design concept in mind, their master tailors bring your vision to life with remarkable accuracy.

Picxlart Photography Note: We make sure that the intricate hand-stitched details of your custom outfit are highlighted prominently in your wedding album.

7. Surbhi Pansari

A prominent favorite among Tollywood celebrities, Surbhi Pansari’s designer studio offers exceptional Indian ethnic wear. Known for signature embroidered sherwanis and luxurious fabrics, this destination lets you celebrate your biggest day with true star-like style.

Picxlart Photography Note: Designer wear from Surbhi Pansari deserves celebrity-level treatment—our expert lighting and framing ensure you look like a star.

8. Smriti Apparels

Smriti Apparels delivers versatile and elegant menswear options tailored for all wedding ceremonies. Combining comfort with aesthetic appeal, their collection ensures that grooms find sophisticated styles suited to diverse cultural celebrations.

Picxlart Photography Note: We help coordinate the best natural and ambient lighting to match the vibrant energy of the attire you choose.
